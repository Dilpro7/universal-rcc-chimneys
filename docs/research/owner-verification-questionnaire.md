# Owner Verification Questionnaire

## Purpose

Use this questionnaire when the owner becomes available. It requests only
information that cannot responsibly be established through independent market,
SERP, or competitor research. Record each answer and supporting document in
the business verification register; an answer is not automatically publishable
until its status is updated there.

## Company, Identity, and Public Representation

1. What exact business name should appear on the website and quotations?
2. What is the legal entity name, if different, and may it be published?
3. What is the current relationship, if any, with KGN Ceramics & Refractories,
   and what wording is accurate and approved for public use?
4. What is the official designation of Md. Ziaullah / Md. Kamaal Pasha?
5. What establishment year, company description, registrations, GSTIN,
   Udyam/MSME details, licences, and certifications can be documented and
   publicly displayed? Please provide source documents.

## Offices and Contact Routes

6. What are the complete current public addresses for the Gujarat head office
   and Vuyyuru office? Are these customer-facing and eligible for local
   business listings?
7. What official phone, email, and WhatsApp routes may be published? Which
   number/channel should receive urgent or quotation enquiries?
8. Are there other active offices or service bases that can be accurately
   represented? What geographic work can genuinely be delivered from each?

## Current Services and Delivery Scope

9. For each historical catalogue service—RCC chimney construction, chimney or
   boiler repair, refractory works, RCC/MS chimney painting, and refractory
   material supply—is it currently offered? State any exclusions.
10. Does the business provide design, structural/engineering work, independent
    material supply, installation, inspection, maintenance, or turnkey delivery?
    Define what is performed in-house versus by others.
11. Which industries, project scales, locations, and enquiry types are priority
    business targets? Which work should the website explicitly not solicit?
12. What technical specifications, standards, methods, or safety claims can be
    substantiated and approved for publication?

## Projects, Images, and Trust Evidence

13. Provide 10–20 recent projects, where possible: client, location, year,
    industry, scope, relevant dimensions/specifications, outcome, and contact
    person for verification.
14. For each project, may the client name, logo, description, location, and
    photographs be published? Document permission and confidentiality limits.
15. Who owns each photograph and can it be used on the website and social/search
    previews? Are before/after or process images available?
16. Which credentials, memberships, quality/safety documents, testimonials, or
    references are current and publication-approved? Do not provide material
    that implies permission where none exists.

## Lead Handling and Measurement

17. How do new customers currently find the business (for example referrals,
    tenders, contractors, consultants, repeat customers, search, or direct
    outreach)? Which sources produce qualified work?
18. Who receives website enquiries, during what hours, and what response
    expectation can the company meet?
19. What minimum information is needed to assess or quote an enquiry? Are files,
    drawings, photographs, site location, or specifications needed at first
    contact?
20. Is WhatsApp preferred? May a contact form collect personal data and optional
    attachments? Where should submissions go, how long should they be retained,
    and is a CRM or spreadsheet already used?
21. Which actions should count as qualified leads (call, WhatsApp, form,
    quotation request, tender invite), and who will review them?

## Completion Record

For each answer, capture: answer date, person confirming it, supporting file or
source, publication permission, resulting status (`VERIFIED`, `HISTORICAL`,
`PENDING`, or `DO NOT PUBLISH`), and the verification-register section to
update. Do not place private documents or personal contact data in this file.

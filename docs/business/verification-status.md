## Purpose

This document tracks the verification state of business information
used by the website project.

Codex and any other agent working on this repository MUST consult this
document before using business information in public-facing website
content.

---

# 1. Verification Status Definitions

## VERIFIED

Information directly confirmed as current by the business or supported
by reliable current business documentation.

Verified information may be used in public website content, subject to
normal editorial review.

---

## HISTORICAL

Information found in an older company catalogue or other historical
material but not independently confirmed as current.

Historical information may be useful for research and internal
context.

It must NOT automatically be presented as a current company fact.

---

## PENDING

Information that is currently unknown or requires confirmation from the
business.

Pending information must not be presented as fact.

---

## DO NOT PUBLISH

Information that should explicitly not appear on the public website
until sufficient verification or authorization exists.

This status should be used for sensitive, legally significant,
potentially misleading, or otherwise restricted information.
